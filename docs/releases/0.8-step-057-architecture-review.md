## 7. Known Step 055 overview / news quality risk

**PROVEN BY** `docs/releases/0.8-step-055-implementation.md`:

With `legacy_doc_type_canonical_enabled=false` (default), an overview-style candidate pool that previously relied on doc-type demotion of news can retain order such as:

**news → about → homepage**

instead of demoting news in favor of about/homepage.

| Classification | Detail |
|----------------|--------|
| Expected | Default-off reorder is intentional Option A |
| Quality risk | Overview answers may cite news more often until Memory authority or ops rollback |
| Golden CI | Unit golden fixtures did not show serious regression (mocked RagResult path) |
| Mitigation | Settings PUT `legacy_doc_type_canonical_enabled=true` (no reindex required for the gate itself) |
| Not a blocker for Engineering Ready | Documented accepted risk; staging must evaluate live corpus before Production Ready |
